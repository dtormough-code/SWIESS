The story

Started with one machine and a folding table

Brew & Co. opened as a pop-up cart before finding its home on 5th Street. The menu changes with what's in season and what the roaster's excited about that month — the constant is that everything's made in-house, in small batches, by the same three people who'll hand it to you.
